## Two entities, not one

The modelling decision that shapes everything else is separating **foods** from
**meals**. A food carries macros per 100g and nothing else. A meal is a list of
foods with quantities.

That separation is what makes a recipe reusable. Enter the ingredients of a dish
once, and logging it later is a single action instead of six. It also means
correcting a food's macros retroactively fixes every meal that uses it, rather
than leaving stale copies scattered through the history.

## The URL is the state

Search terms, filters and the selected columns are all held in the URL rather
than in component state. The effect is that any view can be shared, bookmarked,
or reopened exactly as it was — and the back button behaves the way people expect
instead of throwing away their filters.

It costs a little more code than `useState`. It buys behaviour that users notice
immediately when it is missing.

## Statistics from the same records

Intake statistics are derived from the logged meals, not accumulated into
separate totals. There is no path where the charts and the history can disagree,
because they are reading the same rows.
